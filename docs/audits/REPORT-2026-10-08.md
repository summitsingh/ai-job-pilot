## New findings

### HIGH — map.py duplicates ACK_BAD_RE with a weaker copy

**Location:** `map.py:603-605`.

`map.py` defines its own `ACK_BAD_RE` (`arbitrat|background.?check|drug.?test|
assessment|criminal|credit.?check|security.?clearance`) used by
`apply_acknowledge_override`. It omits `true and correct`, `certif`, *and*
`attest` — weaker than even the pre-fix templates.py version. The two copies
have now diverged (templates.py was partially fixed; map.py was not), so the
override path remains fully exposed to certification/attestation auto-clicks.

**Fix:** Delete the map.py copy; import `ACK_BAD_RE` from templates.py so there
is one denylist. Then add `attest` to it.

### MEDIUM — Sponsorship regex branches are inconsistent

**Location:** `templates.py:708-709`, `templates.py:857-858`.

The "authorized to work" pattern and its guard exemption apply the
`without sponsorship` lookahead to only one of two regex branches, and only for
the literal phrase. Centralize into one helper (e.g. `sponsorship_excluded(label)`)
covering "without sponsorship", "without requiring sponsorship", "no
sponsorship", and apply it uniformly.
